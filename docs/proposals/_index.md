# Proposals

Collects candidate designs for the open goals of `ekthesis`, so that a decision can weigh them.

- [Proposals for the assembly schema](assembly-schema.md): weighs how an argument marks its core ideas, and how its views and announcements follow from that mark
- [Proposals on the order of ideas](order-of-ideas.md): weighs how the reading order of an exposition follows from its relations and from the strategy of each part
- [Proposals for the specification of an argument](argument-specification.md): weighs the additions to the record and to the check of an exposition that let a checker test the function of each idea before the prose
