# Proposals

Collects candidate designs for the open goals of `ekthesis`, so that a decision can weigh them.

> [!WARNING] Proposals carried whole from `gnomon`
> Three proposals still hold the designs that stay in `gnomon`: the relations between research units, the gaps and the weakest link of an assembly, and the concept record. Those designs are removed when each proposal is revised.

- [Proposals for the exposition family](exposition-family.md): weighs the records, moves and relations of an exposition
- [Proposals for the relation registry](relation-registry.md): weighs how a relation of an exposition to the units of its source is declared and checked
- [Proposals for the assembly schema](assembly-schema.md): weighs how a component is marked as core or elaboration within one argument
- [Proposals for the vocabulary records](vocabulary-records.md): weighs the records of a glossary and of an audience
