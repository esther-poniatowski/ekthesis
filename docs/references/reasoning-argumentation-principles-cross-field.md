# Reasoning principles across fields

## Objective

Several [theories and research fields](reasoning-argumentation-principles-by-field.md) establish the principles of validity, reasoning, explanations, understanding versus logical validity. The current classification moves from a **field-by-field inventory** to a **cross-domain taxonomy**.

## Overlaps and distinctions

### Abstraction levels

Appropriate abstraction is a balance principle. This principle is irreducible. It mediates between two symmetrical failures:

- too much low-level detail, producing overload
- too much abstraction, erasing the real dependency structure
