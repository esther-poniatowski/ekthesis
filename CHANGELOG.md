# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- The rule `distinct-keys` of `ekthesis check` reports a key that a mapping of a record repeats, at the repeated key, whatever the contract of the record. The YAML parser keeps the last of two equal keys and drops the earlier value, so the rule reads the node tree of the record.
- `ekthesis check` and `ekthesis scope-table` take a folder in place of files: a folder stands for the YAML files that it holds directly, in name order.
- The contract of an audience record, with its schema, its skeleton `data/skeletons/audience.yml`, and its export in the configuration of Kleisis. Its first version, `ekthesis:audience@1`, wrote its logical targets as text; it is retired, since every record has migrated to `ekthesis:audience@2`.
- The contract `ekthesis:constraints@1` of the constraints that a venue imposes on one kind of exposition, with its schema, its skeleton `data/skeletons/constraints.yml`, and its export in the configuration of Kleisis. A record declares its sources once, and each constraint states its requirement and the sources that state it, with an optional group.
- `ekthesis check` checks constraints records: after the schema, the rule `unique-identifiers` checks the identifiers within each of their groups, sources and constraints, and the new rule `unique-units` reports an identifier shared by two of these collections, since the units of one record share one namespace.
- The field `venue-constraints` of `ekthesis:exposition@3` accepts the logical target of a constraints record, typed by `x-target-contract: ['ekthesis:constraints']` and naming no unit, beside the link that it held before; every valid record stays valid, so the contract keeps its version. Anagraphe resolves the target and reports a record of another contract.
- `ekthesis check` checks audience records: the rules `unique-audiences` and `acyclic-parents` check the audience records of one run together, and the rule `undeclared-concepts` reports each undeclared concept as a warning.
- The command `ekthesis scope-table` writes the scope table `onomasia:scope-table@1` of a set of audience records, and its option `--check` reports, under the rule `scope-table-current`, a written table that differs from the derived one. When some records hold an error, the command derives no table, and reports at the table one finding of the rule `scope-table-derivable` naming the number of those records, whose errors `ekthesis check` reports.

### Changed

- The skeletons `abstract.md` and `claim-boundaries.md` name no relation, role, task, field or vocabulary of Gnomon: each names the source of a question, a condition or a ground generically. The guidance specific to Gnomon moves to the starter files of the consumer.
- **Breaking:** `ekthesis scope-table` names the contract of the table in its field `_schema`, instead of `contract`, as `onomasia:scope-table@1` binds its documents since Onomasia `eb2958c`. The option `--check` compares the binding, the fields and the rows with the derived table, and no longer checks the structure of the field `sources` against a model of its own: the gate validates the table against the schema of Onomasia. A table written before names its contract under `contract`, so `--check` reports it until it is written again. The tests validate a written table, unmodified, against the schema of Onomasia, and run the cases of the field `sources` of Onomasia against the provenance of Ekthesis, both at the commit that `config/kleisis.yml` pins.
- **Breaking:** the configuration of Kleisis moves from `specs/schema-resources.yml` to `config/kleisis.yml`, of format `kleisis:configuration@1`, which Kleisis finds in the place `config/` of the root, as the [specification of Hodegos](https://github.com/esther-poniatowski/hodegos/blob/main/docs/specification.md) states. The configuration declares the keywords `x-registry` and `x-role`, the only keywords to which Ekthesis gives a meaning, and the registry `specs/schema-registry.yml` records them. The configuration names three dependencies: Anagraphe, for the addressing schema and the keywords `x-identifier` and `x-target-contract`; Ekphrasis, for the keyword `x-kind`; and Onomasia, for the contract `onomasia:scope-table@1`. A consumer that read the former record reads the configuration instead.
- `ekthesis` prints, converts and matches its field paths through `anagraphe-read`, a new dependency, pinned by commit in `environment.yml`. A text line prints a field path as the field-path specification of Anagraphe prints it: a key that a bare key cannot hold, such as a key holding a space or a quote, is written in brackets as a JSON string with its escapes. The JSON Pointer of a locus is unchanged.
- The schemas of `ekthesis:exposition@3`, `ekthesis:exposition@1` and `ekthesis:constraints@1` drop the keyword `x-refers`. A field that names an item of its record, such as the source and the target of a relation, the ideas of a part, and the group and the sources of a constraint, lists under `x-units`, the keyword of Anagraphe, the arrays of the record whose items it may name, and takes the type of the identifier of those items. The check reads `x-units` only to find the collection of the ideas, and skips a declaration that locates no collection, such as a malformed one or one inside a branch, whose report belongs to Anagraphe. The validation of every record is unchanged, so each contract keeps its version.
- **Breaking:** the contract of an audience record becomes `ekthesis:audience@2`. The fields `identity.representatives[].profile` and `knowledge[].concept` hold logical targets in the form of Anagraphe, a mapping `{repository, id, unit}`: a profile names no unit, and leaves the contract of its record open for the consumer to narrow, and a concept is untyped. The fields `identity.narrows[]` and `identity.confusions[].community` also hold logical targets, naming an audience record of the repository by its id alone, such as `{id: neuroscientists}`: a parent is typed by `x-target-contract: ['ekthesis:audience@2']`, and a community by `['ekthesis:audience']`. The schema references the target of Anagraphe by its absolute `$id`; the check reads that schema from `anagraphe-read`, and the configuration of Kleisis selects Anagraphe as a source of resources only. A record migrates by naming `ekthesis:audience@2` and writing each target as a mapping, such as `profile: {id: doe_jane}` or `narrows: [{id: neuroscientists}]`. No release has published `ekthesis:audience@2`, so the targets of its parents and communities change it in place.
- **Breaking:** the contract of an argument record becomes `ekthesis:exposition@3`. It drops the field `exports` of the exposition and the field `unit` of an idea, and requires the field `kind` on every idea: a referenced idea states the kind of its unit, outside the registry of authored kinds, and an idea without a `source` is authored. The `source` of a referenced idea is a logical target of Anagraphe, such as `{id: mixture-input, unit: definition}`, whose existence `anagraphe targets check` verifies. `ekthesis:exposition@1` stays in the package and in the exports of the configuration of Kleisis until every record has migrated, and `ekthesis check` applies the same rules to its records. A record migrates by naming `ekthesis:exposition@3`, stating the kind of each referenced idea, and writing each source as a logical target.
- The rule `relation-requirements` reports each relation between two referenced ideas that requires a recorded relation as unverified, since `ekthesis` reads no source, and names the kind that each idea states.
- `ekthesis check` takes the root of the paths of a SARIF log from `-r`, `--root`, then from `EKTHESIS_ROOT`, then from the working directory, by the command-line conventions of the workspace.
- `ekthesis check` accepts the short form `-o` of `--output`, and writes the text lines to that file as well as the SARIF log.
- `ekthesis check` applies to each record the rules of its contract: the rules on argument records to `ekthesis:exposition@3` and `ekthesis:exposition@1`, the rules on audiences to `ekthesis:audience@2`, and the schema alone to a contract without rules. A record that breaks its schema skips the rules of its contract, and a record that names no known contract skips the rules of every contract.
- `environment.yml` pins `anagraphe-read` 0.7.0 at Anagraphe `646b9d1`, Kleisis `b098f5e` and Elenchos `33160b2`, each of which requires Hodegos `a151a28`, so that the sibling pins install together. `config/kleisis.yml` pins Anagraphe at the same commit, which declares the keyword `x-units`, and whose addressing schema keeps its bytes, so the registry is unchanged. The interface of `anagraphe_read` that Ekthesis imports, and `elenchos.profile`, are unchanged.

### Removed

- The validator of the findings profile written in the tests, `tests/test_ekthesis/sarif_profile.py`, and its reading of the test vectors in the sibling clone of Elenchos. The tests check each log with the interface `elenchos.profile` of Elenchos, pinned by commit in `environment.yml`, which holds no rule requiring `isComprehensive` to be true.
- The root `hodegos.yml`, which declared the folder `config/tools/` to Hodegos. Hodegos `a151a28` reads no declaration, and searches `config/` as a place of each folder. The tests pin Hodegos `a151a28`.
- The copy of the addressing schema of Anagraphe under `data/resources`, whose bytes differed from those of Anagraphe under the same `$id`. The check reads the schema from `anagraphe-read`, at the commit from which Kleisis materializes the same resource.
- **Breaking:** the rule `source-links` and the module `ekthesis.links`. `ekthesis check` resolves no link to a source, since Anagraphe checks the destination of each link, and admits no heading anchor that Anagraphe rejects. The schema of `ekthesis:exposition@1` drops the keyword `x-resolves`, and keeps its validation.
- **Breaking:** the contract `ekthesis:export@1` of a research export, its schema `export.schema.yaml` and the module `ekthesis.exports`, retired by the decision of `geonexus` on independent research and exposition. the configuration of Kleisis no longer exports the contract.
- **Breaking:** the rule `existing-audiences`, which reported a parent or a confused community naming no audience record of the run. `anagraphe targets check` reports such a target as `unknown-record`, and a record of another contract as `target-contract-mismatch`; the rule leaves the rule list of the SARIF log. `ekthesis scope-table` leaves out a parent that names no audience record of its run.
- **Breaking:** the rule `existing-references`, which reported a field naming no item of its record, on argument records and constraints records. `anagraphe targets check` reports such a value as `unknown-unit-reference`, by the keyword `x-units` of the field, and `ekthesis check` checks no such value; the rule leaves the rule list of the SARIF log.
- **Breaking:** the rules `export-conformance`, `existing-units` and `recorded-relations`, which read the exports that a record named; they leave the rule list of the SARIF log.

### Fixed

- `ekthesis -v` and `ekthesis --version` print the version, where they ended with the error of a missing command.
